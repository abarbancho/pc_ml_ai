Module 5:

- assignment5_1: Will the Customer Accept the Coupon?

Analysis 1:
Base on this analysis, we can conclude that customers who accepted bar coupons have the following characteristics:
 1. They normally is going to bars more than 3 times a week.
 2. They are young people, but not too young, between 25 and 30 years old.
 3. They usually are not traveling with kids.
 4. They don't frequent cheap restaurants and usually have an income higher than $50K
 5. They are not widowed most probably.
 
Analysis 2:
We can conclude from the analysis above that customers who accepted coffee house coupons have the following characteristics:
 1. They are younger people, mostly below 21 years old.
 2. Gender is not determinative.
 3. They are more likely to have lower incomes, but the trend is not very strong.
 4. Person who go to coffee house more than 3 times a week and are below 21 have higher acceptance rate.
